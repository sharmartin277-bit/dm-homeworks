Common Questions

Answered the way we'd answer at your kitchen table.

Do you take small jobs, or only large remodels?

Both. Repairs are a large part of what we do. The family you trust to patch a wall this year is the family you will call for a kitchen next year — that is how we have always earned work.

How much will my project cost?

Every home is different, so we price work after seeing it in person. Your written estimate will show the full scope and price before work begins, so you can decide with clear eyes.

Will my home be a mess during the work?

We protect floors, contain work areas, and clean up as we go. Most of our work happens in homes where families are still living, cooking, and sleeping — we plan around your life, not over it.

Why choose a family company over a large franchise?

Accountability. With a franchise, you get whoever is assigned. With D&M, the family's name is on the business, the truck, and the work — and we intend to keep that name worth trusting.
